HOW SCORING WORKS

Match result recording

Referees record the two sides' scores and boards played. The winner is the higher-scoring side, unless a walkover is recorded.

Sb = (winner points − loser points) / boards played

Changes are synchronized through the tournament database when connected. A completed final determines a champion; Round 1 winners are not labelled champions.
